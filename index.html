About Me

When I was 10, my favorite show was Dance Moms. I would dance infront of the TV with my dust-stained socks and begged my parents for ballet slippers (they said no). Once I had enough of begging, I grabbed every tool at my disposal, and created a pair of dance shoes with a roll of duct tape. After that, I wore them every morning while I'd watch (until I realized I wanted to be Abby Lee Miller and made my little brother one of the dancers). So what does this all mean? Well, I wanna work on projects that I'd use myself and that would let me be creative and challenge me to think about things in a new way.
